# Demo walkthrough

## For reviewers

1. [Open the hosted demo](https://35-226-89-51.sslip.io/) in a desktop browser. No invite code is needed. One participant can run a study at a time.
2. Begin the interview and allow the camera and microphone. Tell Mira what kind of clothing you want.
3. Open the store from Magic Canvas, then share that store tab/window. Keep the call tab open.
4. Explore naturally and think aloud. When something is difficult, explain the problem and what would help. Examples of small changes are clearer measurements, saved items, or a more useful filtering control. These are examples, not scripted patch triggers.
5. Complete the simulated checkout. Mira asks about your experience and a concern from the synthetic knowledge base while accepted changes finish.
6. When invited, reload the **store tab**, try the changes, and give feedback on each one. Do not reload the interview tab: that ends the study.
7. Open the report to review each friction point, its clip, quotes, fix, and the full timeline.

## What to notice

- Questions connect to the observed task and ask the participant to explain their experience.
- The live worker changes source code; it is not switching between two predetermined versions.
- Planning, coding, and verification are observable stages. A revision is not published merely because the model says it is done.
- The same participant evaluates the revision, so the report can preserve before/after feedback.

## Expectations

This is a prototype with external model and video services. The worker accepts at most two changes per study; complicated suggestions may need to be reduced to a small UI change. Timing varies and failed verification blocks publication. Camera cues are uncertain, and spoken questions may occasionally need clarification.

Clips save automatically around confirmed friction; the entire call is not recorded by default. The checkout is fictional. The knowledge-base counts are synthetic demonstration data, not real research findings.

The hosted demo may be unavailable while another study is active or infrastructure is offline. Source review and automated tests do not require starting a paid video conversation.
